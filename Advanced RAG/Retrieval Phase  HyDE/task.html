Description

So far, we’ve used prompt rewriting techniques to enhance the user’s query. Now, we are going to use HyDE (Hypothetical Document Embeddings) to further enhance retrieval. With HyDE, you use an LLM to generate a “hypothetical document” based on the user’s query, similar to what you’d obtain from the vector store. Then, you need to embed the generated document and use it to retrieve real documents instead of using the user’s original query. The hypothetical embedding is likely to contain more relevant keywords than the user’s query, which means you are more likely to receive more relevant results.

You can read more about HyDE in the research paper.

When a user asks a question, prompt the LLM to come up with a probable document that would be retrieved to answer the question. In your prompt, you need to ask the LLM to generate a short summary emphasizing keywords that would most likely be found in a real document on that topic. Then, use the summary to find the top most relevant chunks. If the query involves using a relational database, the LLM should include keywords to ensure the right tool is called.

Objectives

To pass the stage:

  • Once a query is received, use an LLM to generate a hypothetical document that would best answer the question;

  • Few-shot the LLM to generate the hypothetical document in the same format as your real documents;

  • Print the contents of the generated hypothetical document;

  • Embed the hypothetical document and use it to retrieve real documents from the vector store (when using LangChain retrievers, embedding is automatically done behind the scenes);

  • Print the contents of the retrieved documents as well as order records.

Examples

The greater-than symbol followed by a space (> ) represents the user input. Note that it's not part of the input.

Example 1.1: Hypothetical support document

> How can I contact support? 
Hypothetical Document:
- question: How can I contact customer support? 
- answer: You can contact our customer support team via email at support@example.com or by calling our toll-free number at 1-800-555-0199. Our agents are available 24/7 to assist you with your inquiries and concerns.
- question: What support channels are available? 
- answer: We offer multiple support channels including live chat on our website, email support, and phone support. Choose the option that works best for you, and we’ll be glad to help!
- policy: Our support team responds to emails within 24 hours. If you require immediate assistance, please reach out through live chat or call our support hotline.
- policy: Customer support is available during holidays. You can always reach us through our online ticketing system, which operates 24/7, for any urgent issues. 
- question: Is there a live chat option for support? - answer: Yes, we provide a live chat feature on our website where you can connect with a support representative instantly during business hours.

Example 2.1: Hypothetical policy document

> I need to track my order
Hypothetical Document:
- question: How can I track my order? 
- answer: You can track your order by visiting our website and entering your order number in the "Order Tracking" section. You will receive real-time updates regarding your shipment status and estimated delivery date. 
- question: What should I do if my tracking information is not updating? 
- answer: If your tracking information is not updating, please contact our customer support team. They will provide you with the latest information and assist you with any issues regarding your shipment.
- policy: Orders are typically processed within 1-2 business days. Once shipped, you will receive a confirmation email with your tracking details. 
- policy: If your order has been delayed, please check your tracking information and allow 1-2 additional business days for updates. 
- policy: For international orders, please be aware that customs and shipping times may vary, affecting your tracking updates.
